Frequently asked questions

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh? +

Yes, absolutely. Over 90% of our clients are based globally across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland, and UAE. We maintain remote-first, async-friendly communication structures to ensure overlapping updates occur smoothly regardless of timezone differences.

How long does it take to see SEO results? +

SEO is a long-term investment. Generally, initial rankings and organic indexing enhancements are observed within 3 to 6 months of launching technical fixes and structural content mapping. Competitive keywords may require sustained clustering cycles over 6 to 12 months to drive top placements.

What is your pricing structure? +

We work primarily on a monthly retainer basis ranging from $299 to $1,199 depending on your niche, keyword volume, and active developer requirements. Custom structural design projects are priced based on precise feature scopes.

Do you offer a free consultation? +

Yes. We offer an initial free 30-minute growth strategy call. We audit your current visibility, target gaps, and structure a custom plan that outlines immediate fixes you can run yourself or contract us to execute.

Can you handle multiple services at once? +

Yes. Our agency offers integrated campaigns combining Technical SEO, Content Strategy, WordPress/Shopify Web Development, and Paid Google/Meta campaigns under a singular reporting dashboard to maximize alignment across your sales funnel.

How do I pay — and what currency? +

Payments are handled securely online via credit cards, bank wire transfers, or direct PayPal billing invoices. Retainers are billed monthly in US Dollars (USD) or equivalent local currencies for convenience.
